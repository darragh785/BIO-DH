01. About Me

Hello! I’m Darragh, an engineering graduate from Cork with a BEng (Hons) in Intelligent Systems Engineering from MTU — First Class Honours, 76%.

My course, formerly Smart Product Engineering, brought together electronics, embedded systems, programming, data and cloud computing. I like the variety: working through a circuit fault one day and exploring a Python application the next.

At Moog, Alcon and Somex, I’ve gained experience in PCB testing, manufacturing equipment and automation. I enjoy learning from people on the floor and working through practical problems together.

My time at MTU also included hurling and volunteering. Away from engineering, I enjoy golf, fishing and travel — there’s a little more of that below.

Final-year team project

SkySpot: Drone-Based Parking Intelligence

A drone-based parking intelligence project, connecting aerial imagery with occupancy analysis and a wider cloud application.

My contribution

My contribution focused on making sense of the flight and image data: inspecting telemetry, aligning frames, working through yaw correction and camera geometry, and developing Python/Jupyter processing for assigning observations to parking bays.

  • Working between physical coordinates, camera views and mapped parking bays.
  • Checking assumptions in data before relying on an output.
  • Bringing one part of a larger engineering system into a team workflow.
